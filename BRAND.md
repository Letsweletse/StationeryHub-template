# StationeryHUB brand

Always use the official logo on anything made for StationeryHUB (flyers, banners, emails, quotes):

- `public/brand/stationeryhub-logo.png` – transparent background (use on dark designs)
- `public/brand/stationeryhub-logo-white-bg.png` – white background (use on light designs)

Logo: red shopping-bag icon, black pill with "Stationery" in white and "HUB" in red.
Never replace it with plain text or an invented icon.
